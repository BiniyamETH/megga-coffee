Our Story

A Local Café with a Big Heart

A Habesha neighbourhood café on Danforth Avenue.

Morning Grace Coffee

Your neighbourhood coffee & breakfast spot

Megga Morning Grace Coffee is a Habesha café at 1696 Danforth Ave in Toronto's Danforth East. We pour espresso drinks and serve a full breakfast and brunch menu, from breakfast sandwiches and benedicts to omelettes, pancakes, burgers, Greek-style plates and vegetarian wraps.

It's a warm, relaxed room to meet friends, work for an hour or start the day slowly. The Danforth Mosaic BIA welcomed us as a new addition to Danforth East, and we're glad to be part of the neighbourhood. Plan your visit.

Our Habesha roots

Coffee, the Habesha Way ቡና

Megga Morning Grace Coffee is a Habesha café, and coffee is close to our hearts. In Habesha homes, sharing a cup with family and neighbours is part of everyday life, and we bring that same warmth to the Danforth. You’ll find Habesha breakfasts like ful, fata and kicha fitfit on our menu.

  • The birthplace of coffee

    Arabica coffee first grew wild in the highland forests of the Horn of Africa. Legend says a goat herder named Kaldi noticed its energizing red cherries.

  • The coffee ceremony

    In both countries, green beans are roasted, ground and brewed in a clay jebena, then served in three rounds, known in Amharic as abol, tona and baraka.

  • ቡና ዳቦ ነው · Buna dabo naw

    “Coffee is our bread.” This Amharic saying shows how central coffee is to daily life, and to good company.
