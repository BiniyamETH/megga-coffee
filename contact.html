What does Habesha mean?

Habesha is the name Ethiopian and Eritrean people share for their common culture, food and coffee traditions. Megga welcomes everyone, from neighbours who grew up with a coffee ceremony to anyone trying ful or tbsi for the first time.

Do you serve Habesha food?

Yes. Our menu has its own Habesha Dishes section: ful (mashed fava beans), fata (bread soaked in a spiced berbere sauce), kicha fitfit, ga’at, and two kinds of tbsi (tibs, sautéed meat). See them on the menu.

Is Megga Morning Grace Coffee a Habesha café?

Yes. Megga is a Habesha neighbourhood café. Coffee is at the heart of Habesha culture, and the Horn of Africa is the birthplace of coffee. Alongside espresso drinks we serve Habesha breakfasts like ful, fata and kicha fitfit and a full breakfast and brunch menu. Read more about our Habesha roots.
