    Why Megga

    Coffee & Breakfast near Coxwell Station

    We're on Danforth Avenue in Toronto's Danforth East, between Coxwell and Woodbine. Stop in on your way to the subway, meet a friend for brunch, or settle in with a latte. We're open every day from 8 a.m. to 9 p.m.

    • Espresso Bar

      Lattes, cappuccinos, mochas and americanos, made to order.

      Coffee menu

    • Breakfast & Brunch

      Eggs Benedict, omelettes, pancakes, breakfast sandwiches and home fries.

      Breakfast menu

    • Vegetarian Options

      Veggie wraps and vegetarian plates, plus gluten-free toast. Look for the leaf icon on the menu.

      Vegetarian dishes

    • Habesha Roots

      Coffee is at the heart of Habesha culture, and the Horn of Africa is where coffee began. Try a Habesha breakfast like ful or fata.
