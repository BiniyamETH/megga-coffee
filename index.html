      Good to Know

      Quick Answers

      Where is Megga Coffee?

      We’re at 1696 Danforth Ave, Toronto, ON M4C 1H8, in Danforth East between Coxwell and Woodbine. Get directions in Google Maps.

      What are your hours?

      We’re open daily from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.

      Do you have vegetarian options?

      Yes. Look for the leaf icon on the menu, or tap the Vegetarian filter. We also offer gluten-free toast.

      Can I work or study at Megga Coffee?

      Yes. We have free Wi-Fi, power outlets, and seating inside and outside. Laptops are welcome any time we’re open.

      Do you offer takeout and delivery?

      Yes. Stop by to order takeout or check Uber Eats for pickup and delivery options.

      Which subway station is closest?

      Coxwell Station on TTC Line 2 (Bloor–Danforth). We’re a short walk east along Danforth Avenue, at number 1696.
